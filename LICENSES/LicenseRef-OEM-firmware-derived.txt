Derived, for interoperability, from the ACPI firmware shipped with the HONOR
MagicBook Pro 14 2025 (FMB-P), with only the minimal changes required to run
Linux on it (see dsdt/prebuilt/fix.global.patch). The original table is
copyright HONOR Device Co., Ltd. These files are redistributed to owners of the
covered hardware as an interoperability fix, in line with the long-standing
community practice of publishing corrected laptop DSDTs. Prefer building the
table from your own firmware with dsdt/build.sh.
